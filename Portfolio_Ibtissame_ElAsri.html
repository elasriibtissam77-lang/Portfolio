PROJECT
Current · Final-Year Project (PFE)

Digital twin for predictive maintenance of ONCF pantographs

In partnership with ONCF — Morocco's national railway operator

Objective

  • Predict pantograph failure before it happens, using a multimodal digital twin fed by simulated and real sensor data.

Role

  • Modelled the 2-DOF pantograph mechanics in Simulink and built the UDP-to-dashboard data pipeline.
  • Designed the MultiDeepSAD anomaly-detection model and the Blender 3D twin animation.

Outcomes

  • Reached 0.9993 AUC combining an FFT signal branch with a CNN-ResNet vision branch.
  • Classified 3 AMDEC fault modes — electric arcing, mechanical crack, pneumatic leak.
  • Delivered live Health Indicator & Remaining Useful Life estimates on a Streamlit dashboard.
